# ai-document-processing-platform
## Overview
This is a self-initiated product case study exploring how AI can streamline document processing and reduce manual effort in business workflows.
## The Problem
Businesses often rely on employees to manually review documents, extract information, and enter data into internal systems. This can be time-consuming and may lead to errors or processing delays.
## Proposed Solution
An AI-powered platform that extracts relevant information from uploaded documents, allows users to review and correct the extracted data, and routes documents through an approval workflow.
## Target Users
Insurance claim employees responsible for incoming claims and supporting documents.
## User Problem
Claims employees often manually review claim forms, extract information such as policy numbers and claim amounts, enter data into internal systems and forward claims for assessment. This process can be time-consuming, repetitive and prone to data-entry errors.
## User Need
Claims employees need a faster way to extract and validate claim information while retaining control over data accuracy.
## Product Goal
Reduce the manual effort required to process insurance claims by using AI to extract relevant information from submitted documents, while ensuring employees can review and correct the results before claims move forward.
## Success Metrics
-**Processing time:** Measure the time required for an employee to prepare a claim for assessment, compared with the existing process.

-**Extraction accuracy:** Measure the percentage of extracted data fields that match the verified information in the source documents.
-**Employee adoption:** Measure the proportion of eligible claims processed through the platform and the proportion of target users actively using it
